Services

You can count on Don Wells Lawn Care to not only meet but exceed all your needs and requests. Learn more about the services we provide below and let us know if you'd like to learn more about a specific offering.

Mowing

Property mowing and string trimming at regular intervals is essential to keeping a lush and beautiful lawn. We offer a variety of plans to meet the needs of your lawn and budget.

Mulch

Mulching is one of the most effective methods of weed control and one of the solutions we typically provide for clients experiencing problems with weeds in their shrub and flower beds. Mulch inhibits weed growth and increases the concentration of nutrients in the soil. We offer both organic and synthetic forms.

Weed Control

When it comes to lawn weed control in Fond du Lac, there's no one-size-fits-all solution. You need to account for type, growing season, and life cycle to take an effective approach. DWLC can identify weeds in your lawn, get them under control, and keep them from coming back.

Lawn Fertilizing

For gorgeous, thick, green grass your lawn needs expert care and proper fertilization. DWLC will provide your lawn with the nutrients it needs to thrive in Wisconsin's climate, adjusting our approach to meet your lawn's exact needs — with our personal guarantee that you'll love the results.

Seasonal Clean Up

Seasonal clean-up is the last thing you want to spend time on after the long Wisconsin winter or just before the snow hits. Leave the spring and fall clean-up to us, and enjoy the changing seasons.

Snow Removal

Fast, reliable plowing, sidewalk shoveling, and salting services. You can trust us to quickly and safely remove snow and ice from parking lots, storefronts, businesses, homes, driveways, and sidewalks.

  • Whatever the conditions, we strive to keep your business open — quickly adjusting to changing weather.
  • Wisconsin winter weather can cause hazardous, slippery areas; we keep them under control with salt.
